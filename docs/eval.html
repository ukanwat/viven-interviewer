How the Viven interviewer is tested

Utkarsh Kanwat · November 2026 · eval/ in the repo

Real conversations can't show what was missed, because nobody knows what the person could have said. So the real interviewer talks to simulated people whose facts I wrote down first, and every prompt change is scored on how many came out and what else got worse.

1The test set

Simulated people10 (5 tune, 5 held back)
Hidden facts104 (56 important)

… only once they trust you22

From a retired principal and a Hinglish-speaking grandmother to a wary ICU nurse and a farmer slow to trust. They get annoyed and can walk out.

5How much to trust these numbers

Different judgeFrom a different company than the interviewer, so it isn't grading its own kind of writing.
Ranges, not single numbersSame people and seeds on both sides. With 5 people per side, small changes come out "can't tell".
Held-back peopleChanges come from failures on 5 people and count only if they help the other 5. Not run yet: the credit ran out.
Checking the judgeGraded twice to see how often labels flip; my marks vs the judge's on a review page. My marks aren't done yet.
Real conversationsNo answer key: the judge writes likely outsider questions, answers them from the notes, checks against the transcript.
Run with node eval/run.ts (sim, grade, compare, view, meta). Grading is cached, so regrading old runs costs nothing. Every round is written up in THINKING.md.
