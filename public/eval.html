Simulated people, each with hidden facts and rules for when they'd share them (freely, if asked, only to a specific why/example, or only once they feel heard). Each talks with the interviewer over text, says "call me later" midway, and comes back. A judge from a different model family checks which facts they actually said (with quotes, verified against the transcript) and what the interviewer got wrong. ours is the product; baseline is the same model with one sensible prompt and the chat history.
